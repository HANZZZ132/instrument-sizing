ESE calculation source notice

The Early Streamer Emission (ESE) protection-radius module was implemented from the user-supplied NF C 17-102, September 2011, Protection against lightning - Early streamer emission lightning protection systems.

Implemented screening rules include:
- Clause 5.2.2: ESEAT efficiency and 60 microsecond maximum.
- Clause 5.2.3.2: protection-radius equations and r values for protection levels I-IV.
- Clause 5.2.3.4: high-rise additional-protection warning.
- Clause 5.2.3.5: optional Level I++ radius reduction warning.
- Clause 5.2.5: minimum 2 m ESEAT tip height above protected area.
- Clause 5.3.2: down-conductor guidance.

The full copyrighted standard is not redistributed with this software.
