NFPA 780 SOURCE NOTICE

Module: Electrical > NFPA 780 Rolling Sphere
Source basis: user-supplied NFPA 780, Standard for the Installation of Lightning Protection Systems, 2000 Edition.

Implemented scope:
- Traditional rolling-sphere geometry screening using the 150 ft (46 m) sphere in Section 3.7.3.
- Section 3.7.3.4 horizontal protected-distance relationship, evaluated in USC units then converted to SI.

Important scope boundary:
- The uploaded NFPA 780 edition explicitly excludes Early Streamer Emission (ESE) systems.
- ESE calculations in this application are separate and are based on the user-supplied NF C 17-102 standard.
- Full NFPA 780 compliance requires project-specific review of strike termination placement, conductor routing, bonding, grounding, surge protection, inspection and maintenance requirements beyond this screening calculator.
