AGA 8 DETAIL REFERENCE NOTICE
=============================
The AGA8 DETAIL calculation engine in aga8_detail.py was implemented for this
application from the publicly available NIST AGA8 reference formulation and
program structure. The original user-supplied aga8.py is retained in the
package but its unfinished DDETAIL/ZDETAIL routines are not used for production
calculation in the UI.

Reference project:
NIST AGA8 supplementary code, AGA 8 Part 1 DETAIL equation of state.
https://pages.nist.gov/AGA8/

Validation values used in self_test.py are published AGA8 program-verification
values and the NIST reference-mixture example.
